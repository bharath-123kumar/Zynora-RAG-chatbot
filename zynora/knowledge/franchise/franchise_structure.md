## Overview
Zyngram operates under a structured dual hierarchy system divided into Physical Hierarchy and Digital Hierarchy to manage administrative, logistical, and network operations efficiently.

## Physical Hierarchy
The physical hierarchy governs retail points, warehousing, logistics nodes, and administrative headquarters.

The physical franchise hierarchy is structured in ascending order as follows:
Point -> Center -> Hub -> Command -> HQ

Detailed breakdown of physical levels:
- Point: The grass-roots retail counter or customer assistance touchpoint directly interacting with end consumers.
- Center: The local neighborhood operational office overseeing multiple Points and managing stock/cash reconciliation.
- Hub: The district fulfillment and logistics warehouse managing distribution across multiple Centers.
- Command: The state or regional administrative command unit handling operations, compliance, and merchant onboarding.
- HQ: The central corporate Headquarters overseeing global governance, technical infrastructure, and strategy.

## Digital Hierarchy
The digital hierarchy governs network routing, cloud resource allocation, data partitioning, and system access levels across the Zyngram digital ecosystem.

The digital franchise hierarchy is structured in ascending order as follows:
Node -> Zone -> Territory -> Region -> Nation

Detailed breakdown of digital levels:
- Node: The edge computing unit or micro-server instance serving individual retail Point terminals.
- Zone: A clustered group of Nodes representing a localized digital network sector.
- Territory: The sub-divisional digital domain managing network traffic and security boundaries across multiple Zones.
- Region: The provincial or state-level digital data infrastructure managing localized databases and RAG indices.
- Nation: The top-level country-wide cloud infrastructure orchestrating global state, security policies, and national sync.
