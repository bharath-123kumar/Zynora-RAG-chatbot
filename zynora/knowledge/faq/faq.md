## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is Zyngram?
Answer: Zyngram is an all-in-one Knowledge Intelligence, commerce, logistics, and digital services ecosystem empowering consumers and franchise networks.

### Q2: What is Zyngram's vision?
Answer: Zyngram's vision is to build an interconnected, inclusive, and friction-free digital economic network that bridges physical enterprise hierarchies with modern digital infrastructure across every town, district, and nation.

### Q3: What is the purpose of the Zyngram platform?
Answer: The purpose of the Zyngram platform is to democratize digital access, drive hyper-local economic growth, deliver instant multi-service commerce (recharge, utility, logistics, e-commerce, jobs), and provide AI-assisted knowledge intelligence across all operational tiers.

### Q4: What is the physical franchise hierarchy?
Answer: The physical franchise hierarchy is: Point -> Center -> Hub -> Command -> HQ.

### Q5: What is the digital franchise hierarchy?
Answer: The digital franchise hierarchy is: Node -> Zone -> Territory -> Region -> Nation.

### Q6: What services are available on Zyngram?
Answer: Zyngram offers Mobile Recharge, Utility Services, E-commerce, Logistics, Food Delivery, Jobs, Freelancing, and Micro-Insurance.

### Q7: What is Mobile Recharge on Zyngram?
Answer: Mobile Recharge enables instant prepaid top-ups, postpaid bill payments, DTH recharges, and data bundle subscriptions across major telecom operators with instant cash-back rewards.

### Q8: What is the Zyngram Code of Conduct?
Answer: The Code of Conduct mandates integrity, professionalism, customer data privacy protection, non-discrimination, and zero tolerance for fraudulent activity.
