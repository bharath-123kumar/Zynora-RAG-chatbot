## Service Overview
Zyngram provides an array of integrated digital services available through its unified mobile and web platform.

## Approved Services

### 1. Mobile Recharge
Mobile Recharge enables instant prepaid top-ups, postpaid bill payments, DTH recharges, and data bundle subscriptions across all major telecom operators. Users receive real-time confirmation receipts and instant cash-back rewards via Zyngram Wallet.

### 2. Utility Services
Utility Services allows seamless payment of electricity bills, water supply bills, piped gas bills, landline telephone charges, and municipal property taxes. All transactions are backed by automated receipt generation and dispute protection.

### 3. E-commerce
Zyngram E-commerce is a hyper-local marketplace connecting local vendors and consumers. Features include daily essentials delivery, electronics, apparel, and direct seller-to-buyer transactions with integrated escrow payment security.

### 4. Logistics
Zyngram Logistics powers last-mile parcel delivery, intra-city courier dispatch, and supply chain tracking. It connects local franchise hubs directly with regional fulfillment centers for same-day and express delivery services.

### 5. Food Delivery
Food Delivery connects users with local restaurants, cloud kitchens, and bakeries. Orders feature real-time GPS delivery tracking, hygiene ratings, and scheduled meal delivery subscriptions.

### 6. Jobs
Zyngram Jobs is a verified employment portal matching local job seekers with nearby business opportunities, entry-level positions, and enterprise vacancies within the Zyngram franchise ecosystem.

### 7. Freelancing
Zyngram Freelancing offers a micro-task and gig marketplace where skilled professionals can offer services such as graphics design, accounting, digital marketing, content writing, and technical repairs to local businesses.

### 8. Other Approved Services
Additional approved services include Zyngram Micro-Insurance, Local Events Ticket Booking, and Community Noticeboard announcements.
