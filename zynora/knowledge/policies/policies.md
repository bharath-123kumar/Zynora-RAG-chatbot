# Zyngram Corporate Policies and Governance Guidelines

## Document Information
- Document ID: KB-POL-001
- Title: Zyngram Business Policies, Code of Conduct, and Operational Rules
- Category: Policies
- Version: 1.0
- Status: APPROVED

## Policy Overview
All employees, partners, franchise operators, and users of Zyngram must strictly comply with official governance policies.

## Official Policies

### 1. Code of Conduct
The Zyngram Code of Conduct mandates integrity, professionalism, non-discrimination, and zero tolerance for fraudulent activity. All staff and franchise representatives must protect customer data privacy, uphold operational transparency, and maintain respectful communication at all times. Prompt reporting of security vulnerabilities or unethical practices is mandatory.

### 2. Promotional Guidelines
Promotional activities across Zyngram networks must rely exclusively on approved brand assets, verified discount vouchers, and official campaign guidelines. Unsolicited spamming, misleading price claims, or unauthorized third-party advertising on Zyngram channels is strictly prohibited.

### 3. Business Policy
Zyngram Business Policy governs merchant onboarding, commission structures, payout settlement timelines (standard T+1 settlement), refund workflows, and partner dispute resolution mechanisms. Franchise owners must maintain required liquidity reserves and adhere to standard operational SLAs.

### 4. User Policy
The Zyngram User Policy defines user account safety guidelines, acceptable platform usage rules, terms of service, and privacy standards. Users are prohibited from sharing credentials, engaging in abusive behavior, or using automated scripts to extract platform data without permission.
